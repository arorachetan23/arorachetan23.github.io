Vision privacy policy

Vision is a personal app used by one person, its owner, on their own computer.

  • What it accesses: the owner's own Gmail, with read-only permission (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete or change mail.
  • Why: to produce personal summaries for the owner, such as spending from bank alert emails.
  • Where data stays: on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared, or sent to third parties, and it is not used for advertising.
  • Other people: Vision has no other users and collects no data about anyone else.
  • Removing access: the owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
